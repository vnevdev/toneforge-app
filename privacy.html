By creating an account or otherwise using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy and our Terms of Service. If you do not agree with this Policy, you must not use the Services. ──────────────────────────────────────── 1. Information We Collect We collect only what the app needs to function. Specifically: 3.1 Account and Authentication Information You create an account in one of three ways: Sign in with Apple. We receive an Apple-issued identifier and your email address. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email" option, we receive only a private relay address and never see your real email. Sign in with Google. We receive your name, email address, and profile picture URL from Google. Email and password. Your password is handled entirely by our authentication provider, Supabase, and is stored only as a salted cryptographic hash. We never receive, see, or store your password in plaintext, and we cannot recover it. 3.2 Profile Information Username, display name, chosen instrument, daily practice goal in minutes, profile photo (if you upload one), your private-profile setting, and whether you identified yourself as a beginner during onboarding. 3.3 Practice and Progression Data Your AMA rating and its history over time, skill tier, current and longest practice streak, date of your last practice session, lifetime practice minutes, count of pieces mastered, and your provisional-rating status. 3.4 Practice Sessions For each session you log: the date, the duration in minutes, and any free-text notes you write. Session notes are free-text fields — please do not enter sensitive personal information in them. 3.5 Tasks, Repertoire, and Lessons Practice tasks (title, category, status, notes, dates, and whether the task was created by you or generated by AI); repertoire entries (piece name, composer, status, and difficulty); AI-generated daily practice plans; AI-generated piece practice breakdowns; and, for beginners, Foundations lesson progress including cached lesson content and whether you passed each knowledge check. 3.6 Social Information Friend relationships and their status (pending or accepted), group challenges you create or join, and your per-challenge practice minutes and best streak. 3.7 AI Consent Record Whether you have permitted us to share practice context with our AI provider, and server-generated timestamps recording when you granted and, if applicable, when you withdrew that permission. We keep this because it is the record of your consent; we cannot rely on your permission without being able to show it. 3.8 AI Usage Metadata For each AI request: the type of request, a timestamp, and a salted SHA-256 hash of your IP address. We use this hash solely to enforce per-user and per-address rate limits that protect the service from abuse. We do not store your raw IP address, and the hash cannot be practically reversed to recover it. 3.9 Information Stored on Your Device Your authentication session tokens are stored encrypted using an AES-256 key held in the iOS Keychain; only the encrypted form is written to app storage. We also store non-sensitive local state on your device, such as cooldown timestamps and cached content, to reduce unnecessary network requests. 3.10 Notifications Notifications are off unless you turn them on. Nothing described in this subsection happens until you accept the iOS permission prompt. What we store. For each device you turn notifications on from, we store an Apple Push Notification service ("APNs") device token, the platform, which APNs environment that token belongs to, and when the record was last updated. We also store your notification settings: the master switch, a separate switch for each kind of notification, the hour you chose for your daily reminder, and your device's time zone as an IANA name such as America/New_York. The time zone is what lets a reminder arrive in your evening rather than ours. It is a clock setting rather than a location fix — it does not tell us where you are, and we use it only to decide when to send. What we queue. Each notification we intend to send is written to a server-side queue as a row holding your account identifier, the kind of notification, its title and body text, the screen it should open, and its delivery status and timestamps. Those rows are how we apply the sending limits described below and how we avoid sending you the same thing twice. They are readable only by you and by our server, and no client can write them. What a notification contains. Practice notifications are assembled from data you have already given us — your instrument, your daily goal, the minutes you logged today, and your current streak. Friend and challenge notifications carry the display name of the person who added you or invited you. We never put your email address, your session notes, your tasks, or your AI coach conversations into a notification. Apple receives this. Delivering a push notification necessarily means handing Apple your device token together with that notification's title and body. Apple is the only route to your device, and its handling of that data is governed by Apple's own privacy policy. Notification content goes to no other third party, and tokens are never used for advertising, never shared with other users, and never sold. How much we send. At most two practice notifications per local day. Friend requests, friend acceptances, and challenge invitations are not counted against that limit, because they are person-to-person and time-sensitive. Nothing is sent between 9:30pm and 8:00am in your local time zone: a practice reminder that comes due inside that window is dropped, and a social one waits until 8:00am. Turning it off, and deletion. You can turn notifications off at any time in Profile → Settings → Notifications, and you can revoke permission entirely in the iOS Settings app. Turning them off in ToneForge deletes that device's token immediately. We also delete a token when Apple tells us it is no longer deliverable — for example because you removed the app from that device — and your tokens, your notification settings, and any notifications still queued for you are deleted when you delete your account. 3.11 What We Do NOT Collect To be explicit, ToneForge does not collect: precise or coarse geolocation (the time zone described in Section 3.10 is a clock setting, not a location fix, and we derive nothing else from it); your contacts; health or fitness data; biometric data; payment or financial information (the app is free and contains no purchases); advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data; audio recordings or microphone input; or camera input. If you upload a profile photo, we access only the single image you select from your photo library — we never browse or upload your library. We do not knowingly collect Sensitive Personal Information, and you should not transmit it through the Services. ──────────────────────────────────────── 4. Artificial Intelligence This Section is the detailed disclosure of how ToneForge uses AI, what leaves our systems, what you are consenting to, and how to stop it. 6.1 You Are Interacting With an AI, Not a Person ToneForge's coach, daily plans, piece breakdowns, and Foundations lessons are generated by an artificial-intelligence system. No human teacher reads your messages or writes the replies. The coach is software. It has no feelings, no memory of you outside the data described below, and no professional qualifications. We tell you this before your first exchange, in the app as well as here, and the coach is instructed never to claim to be human. 6.2 The Provider and the Model AI features are powered by OpenAI, L.L.C. ("OpenAI"), using the GPT-5 model. OpenAI acts as our service provider / processor for this purpose. All AI requests are made from our secure server, never from your device. The API key never ships inside the app, and your device never contacts OpenAI directly. 6.3 Your Consent Is Required, and It Is Separate We do not send anything to OpenAI unless you have separately and explicitly agreed to it. During onboarding you are shown a dedicated screen — not a checkbox buried in these documents — that names OpenAI, itemises exactly what would be transmitted, and asks you to choose. Declining is a real option: practice logging, tasks, rings, streaks, the calendar, friends, leaderboards, and challenges all work without AI. Your decision is recorded on your profile (ai_consent) together with a server-generated timestamp. Our server checks that record on every single AI request and refuses the request if permission is absent or has been withdrawn. 6.4 Exactly What We Send When you use an AI feature, we transmit only the context needed to produce a useful, level-appropriate response: - Your display name, instrument, and daily practice goal - Your AMA rating, skill tier, current streak, and the date you last practiced - The names and composers of pieces in your repertoire - Summary statistics about your recent sessions (counts and averages only) - The titles and categories of your incomplete tasks - For the AI coach, the messages in that conversation We do not transmit your email address, your password, your raw IP address, your friends list, your session notes, your location, or any information about other users. All text you supply is wrapped in explicit delimiters and passed as data, never as instructions, so that user-supplied content cannot alter the AI's behavior. 6.5 What OpenAI Does and Does Not Do With It Your inputs are sent under OpenAI's API terms, which provide that: - Data submitted through the API is not used to train or improve OpenAI's models. - Inputs and outputs may be retained by OpenAI for up to 30 days to provide the service and to detect abuse, after which they are deleted, unless OpenAI is legally required to retain them. We do not sell AI inputs or outputs, do not use them for advertising, and do not use them to train any model of our own. 6.6 Withdrawing Your Consent You may withdraw permission at any time from Profile → Settings → AI features. Withdrawal takes effect on the next request — not the next session — because the check is server-side and re-read every time. After withdrawal, no further data is sent to OpenAI. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand, and it does not delete AI-generated content already saved to your account, which you can delete yourself. Turning AI off does not disable the rest of the app. 6.7 Limits of AI Output — Please Read AI output may be inaccurate, incomplete, or unsuitable. It is generated by a probabilistic model and is not professional musical instruction, medical advice, physical-therapy advice, mental-health care, or a substitute for a qualified teacher or clinician. You remain responsible for evaluating any practice suggestion before acting on it, and for stopping any activity that causes pain or discomfort. The coach is not a counsellor and must not be used as one. It is scope-restricted to music practice. If a message indicates a risk of self-harm, ToneForge does not send that message to OpenAI at all — it is stopped on your device — and the app responds with crisis-support resources instead. Those messages are not logged, not stored, and not transmitted anywhere. If you are in crisis, please contact a local emergency number or a crisis line rather than an app. 6.8 Automated Decisions and Profiling ToneForge calculates your AMA rating, tier, streaks, and ring progress automatically from data you enter. This is automated processing, and we describe it plainly so you can judge it: - It affects only your experience inside ToneForge — what the app suggests you practice and where you appear on a voluntary leaderboard. - It produces no legal effects and no similarly significant effects on you. It is not used for any decision about credit, finance, housing, insurance, employment, education enrolment, healthcare, or access to any essential service, and we do not use it, or any AI output, to make such decisions. - We do not sell it, and we do not use it to profile you for advertising. - The rules are published in these documents rather than hidden: lower ratings gain faster, inactivity decays a rating, and the first seven days count triple. Because it does not produce legal or similarly significant effects, the opt-out rights that attach to automated decision-making under the CCPA/CPRA's ADMT rules, Article 22 of the GDPR, and comparable state statutes are not triggered. You may still contact us with any question or objection about how a rating was reached, and a human will look at it. 6.9 Age AI features are available only to account holders, who must be at least 13 (see Section 12). We do not knowingly provide AI features to children under 13. ──────────────────────────────────────── 7. How We Share Information We share Personal Information only as described here. Information Visible to Other Users ToneForge includes social features. Please read this section carefully. Public profiles (the default). If your profile is not set to private, any signed-in ToneForge user can see your username, display name, instrument, AMA rating, streak, lifetime practice minutes, pieces mastered, profile photo, and join date. Private profiles. If you enable "Private profile" in your settings, only users you have accepted as friends can see the information above. Other users can still find you by username and see your display name and profile photo, so that they are able to send you a friend request — similar to a private social-media account. Profile photos are stored in a public storage bucket. This means a profile photo you upload is reachable by its direct URL by anyone who has that URL, even if your profile is set to private. If this concerns you, do not upload a profile photo. Friends and challenges. Accepted friends can see your practice statistics on your shared leaderboard. Participants in a group challenge can see the practice minutes and best streak of other participants in that challenge. Notifications name you. If you send a friend request, accept one, or add someone to a group challenge, and that person has notifications turned on, the notification delivered to their device contains your display name. Always private. Your email address, your session notes, your tasks, your AI coach conversations, your daily practice plans, your Foundations lesson progress, your notification settings, and your time zone are never visible to other users. ──────────────────────────────────────── 9.
